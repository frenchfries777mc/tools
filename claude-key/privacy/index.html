What the connector does

After you sign in with your Frenchfries777 account, Claude can list, read, search and write the documents you saved in Frenchfries777 Tools (Docs, Sheet, Slides, Blocks, Code, Draw, Score, Video).

What we access

  • Your Frenchfries777 account id and password, only at sign-in. The password is checked by our sign-in service and is never shown to Claude.
  • Your Tools key, which links your account to your saved documents.
  • Only the documents you ask Claude to work with. Nothing else is read.

What we store

  • The connector itself keeps no database. Access and refresh tokens are sealed inside the tokens, so nothing about them is stored on our side.
  • Your documents stay in the Frenchfries777 Tools store, as they did before you connected Claude. When Claude overwrites a document, the previous text is kept once as a backup copy in your own store.

What we share

Document text that Claude reads goes to Claude, in the conversation you started. We do not sell your data and do not send it to anyone else.

Your choices

  • Remove the connector in Claude settings at any time. Access stops when its tokens expire (access: 1 hour, refresh: 30 days).
  • Delete any document inside Frenchfries777 Tools, or ask us to delete your account data.

Frenchfries777 Tools for Claude

Let Claude read, search and edit the documents you made in Frenchfries777 Tools.

Claude가 Tools에서 만든 문서를 읽고 고쳐 줘요.

What it can do

  • list_documents shows your saved documents (Docs, Sheet, Slides, Blocks, Code, Draw, Score, Video).
  • read_document reads one document. Long ones come in parts.
  • search_documents finds documents that contain a word.
  • write_document replaces a document or creates a new one. The old text is kept once as a backup.

Setup

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add Frenchfries777 Tools.
  2. Sign in with your Frenchfries777 account (id and password).
  3. The first time, paste your Tools key. Get it at frenchfries777.com/tools/claude-key/.

Authentication

OAuth 2.1 with PKCE (S256) and dynamic client registration. Access tokens last 1 hour and refresh tokens 30 days.

Examples

  1. "List my Fry Sheet documents." Claude calls list_documents for Sheet and shows the names and sizes.
  2. "Find my slides that mention the school trip." Claude calls search_documents, then read_document on the matches and summarizes them.
  3. "Fix the typos in my Fry Docs essay." Claude reads the document, then calls write_document with the corrected text. The previous version is kept as a backup.

Limits

One document is up to 4 MB and your store is up to 16 MB. Reads come in parts of 60,000 characters. Searches look at up to 40 documents.
